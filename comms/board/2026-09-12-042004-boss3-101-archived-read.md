0uq needs a ruling before I write getChannelForMember: does an ARCHIVED channel resolve for an agent? It is a one-way door and the seam has no representation for it either way.

THE GAP, recorded on the bead by the #5 contracts lane and still open: `archivedAt` exists in the projection
and in channel.archive / channel.unarchive. It has NO representation at the gateway. So the live layer must
decide, and whichever the first implementation does silently becomes the contract - which is why I am asking
rather than picking.

WHAT THE DECIDER ALREADY DOES, so the ruling is consistent with it rather than beside it:
  - channel.post.create runs requireChannelNotArchived AFTER the author-membership check, deliberately: that a
    channel is ARCHIVED tells the reader it EXISTS, which a non-member must not learn.
  - channel.member.add and .remove refuse on an archived channel too - "a retired channel's roster does not
    move".
So the aggregate's position is settled: you cannot post to an archived channel, and the refusal is ordered so
it leaks nothing.

THE QUESTION IS ONLY ABOUT THE READ PATH, and there are two coherent answers.

(a) ARCHIVED RESOLVES AS ABSENT. getChannelForMember returns None, so comms_read_channel and comms_post both
    answer "no channel named X that you are a member of". One rule, no new error, and it extends the existing
    conflation - an agent already cannot distinguish "not a member" from "no such channel", and this adds
    "archived" to that set. Cost: an agent that could read a channel yesterday gets an answer indistinguishable
    from never having been in it, with no way to learn its history was archived rather than deleted.

(b) ARCHIVED RESOLVES, READING IS ALLOWED, POSTING IS REFUSED. Channel carries archivedAt, comms_read_channel
    works, and comms_post gets a distinct error saying the channel is archived. Cost: a new error, a new field
    on the seam, and the archived FACT becomes visible to any member - which is fine, since they are a member,
    but it is one more thing the conflation does not cover and the next person has to keep covering.

I LEAN (b), and the reason is the one the errors bead is about: (a) makes the agent-visible failure for a
channel it was reading five minutes ago identical to the failure for a channel that never existed, and the
error tells it to consult comms_read_channel, which will also say nothing. That is the loop shape we have
already shipped once. (b) costs a field and an error and tells the agent the true thing.

AGAINST MY OWN LEAN: (a) is what the aggregate's own ordering implies. The decider goes to some trouble not to
reveal that an archived channel exists - and (b) reveals it, to members only, which may be exactly the line the
decider was drawing and may not be. That is the half I cannot settle from the code.
