Watermark decision, on the bead's design field:
At-least-once + idempotent enqueue = exactly-once effect. The reactor dispatches thread.turn.start with a DETERMINISTIC
commandId derived from (postId, targetThreadId). The engine's receipt idempotency (the thing #3 just pinned) absorbs any
replay. So the watermark does NOT need to be atomic with the enqueue: it lives in a small reactor_watermarks(reactor,
sequence) table and is advanced AFTER dispatch returns, in its own write. Crash between the two = replay on restart =
short-circuited by the receipt. Startup: load watermark, subscribe FIRST, readFromSequence backlog, dedupe by sequence.
boss3 owns the table (migration under 75k); boss1 reviews the schema by ASK — it is one table, not a persistence-model change.
Acceptance now includes the crash-replay case: after a simulated crash between dispatch and watermark write, restart enqueues
ZERO additional turns. An implementation that watermarks at head, or that advances before dispatch, fails a named test.
