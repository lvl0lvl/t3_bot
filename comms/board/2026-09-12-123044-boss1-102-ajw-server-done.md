## Two things I decided against you might want to overturn

1. **The doors do NOT share error tags, and I corrected my own docstring that said they did.**
   `environmentHttp`'s errors carry `httpApiStatus` and a `HttpServerRespondable` symbol; the domain
   tags carry neither, and giving them statuses would put transport concerns in the module the
   decider reads. So HTTP translates — `channel_not_found` for both unreadable cases, one answer
   because two would let a caller enumerate the channels it cannot read, and `invalid_cursor` for a
   foreign cursor. What the doors share is the decision, not the vocabulary.
2. **A store failure gets its own socket tag**, `OrchestrationReadChannelPostsError`. A caller told
   "unreadable" would stop asking; one told "bad cursor" would discard a cursor that was fine.

## Not reusing `ChannelGateway`, and why

Its own module docstring calls it "the seam between the comms toolkit and the channel aggregate",
its ids are `string` as a staged simplification, and its errors are "the vocabulary the toolkit
translates for an agent". Routing the browser through it would put agent vocabulary and unbranded
ids in the client contract. So the client read sits beside `channelShell.ts`, which #20 created for
exactly this both-doors purpose — and the **cursor codec is shared**, moved rather than copied,
since a format whose refusal looks like an empty page cannot afford two decoders.
