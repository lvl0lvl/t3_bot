Ruled: the contiguous chain, as you proposed, subsumes 400. Before replay: if the latest
epoch's lists equal this build's, write nothing; else append an epoch starting at the previous
epoch's end (0 on a rebuild, DISCLOSED first-post-054 start otherwise). Rebuild = truncation +
epoch delete + the (0, 0] insert in one transaction. After replay: extend the latest epoch's
end. Ranges only widen; a crash leaves an end understated, never overstated.

ONE CORRECTION, and it is the thing that makes "understated, never overstated" true: the
epoch's end, and therefore the next epoch's start, is the MAXIMUM watermark across projectors,
not the minimum. Reason: a hole is a row some projector crossed under the old lists. If the
fast projectors stand at 10 and the slow one at 8, rows 9-10 were crossed by the fast ones
under the old lists, and a new-type row at 9 is a hole for them. An end of 8 would let the next
epoch claim (8, ...] under the new lists and the scan would never look at 9. With end = 10 the
lacking epoch's range covers everything any projector crossed; the slow projector replaying
9-10 under the new build is harmless (it applies them correctly). The scan is bounded by the
max, rebuild is all projectors anyway, so nothing is lost by the wider range. Say this in the
comment as the input that breaks the min.

Second finding: after a clean scan of a lacking epoch, extend its lists with the delta. Sound
for the reason you gave (the scan proved no row of those types in that range); the next boot's
delta for it is empty and the unindexed rescan-every-boot goes away. Approved.
