THE DECISION: channel events are a ONE-WAY DOOR for M1. Once an environment writes one, it cannot be
downgraded to a pre-channel binary without failing to start. Documented in docs/internals as part of yyd,
no machinery added.

WHY, briefly: M1 is one binary on one machine. AGENTS.md says design for current requirements only. The
durable alternative is real machinery on a path every aggregate shares, and buying it now to protect a
scenario we do not have is the "machinery because it looks architecturally impressive" Theo warns about.
REOPENS THE MOMENT channels ship anywhere with independently-upgrading environments — upstream, a relay or
tunnel host, a desktop build a user can roll back. That is a genuine one-way door and it should be crossed
deliberately, not discovered.

NOW THE PART THAT MATTERS — MY ORIGINAL BEAD OFFERED A FIX THAT DOES NOT FIX IT.
I wrote: "advertise a `channels` capability on the environment descriptor and negotiate it the way
pull-request linking does (precedent: environment.ts:127-132, table at overview.md:21-38)."
That is a category error and I only caught it because I went to read the precedent before implementing it
rather than after.
Capabilities are hardcoded in the server's OWN descriptor — ServerEnvironment.ts:215-240, literally
`threadPullRequests: true` — and are sent to CLIENTS over RPC to say what this server supports. NOTHING in
the replay path consults them. A server advertising `channels: false` still has to decode whatever is
already sitting in its own SQLite when it boots, and still dies on the row.
The capability pattern solves CLIENT-vs-SERVER skew. zix is an OLD-BINARY-READS-ITS-OWN-NEW-ROWS problem.
Different axis. Implementing the flag would have looked like a fix, passed review, and left the failure
exactly where it was.

There are only two real options, and I have recorded both on the bead: make the event-store decode tolerant
(skip or quarantine unknown rows — the durable fix, and where ForwardCompatibleArray would belong), or accept
the one-way door and write it down. I took the second for M1 and named the condition that forces the first.
