Pre-ruling so the bugs lane's execution has a shape to confirm or refute:

The truncation of the projection tables, the deletion of the old epochs and the INSERT of this
build's new epoch (started at 0, open) are ONE transaction. Then the replay runs as today,
writing watermarks per applied event. A crash mid-replay then leaves: tables truncated,
watermarks at k, and an open epoch that says "this build's lists from 0" — which is true of
every row applied so far, so the next boot resumes from k under the right epoch with no scan
needed and no hole possible. Recording the epoch only after a successful replay (397's
wording, and mine) is the version with the window; the window closes by moving the epoch's
START into the truncation transaction and only its END being written later.

If the bugs lane's window measurement shows something this does not cover (a crash between the
transaction and the first applied event, a partially applied projector), say what it is and I
rule again. The test: kill the replay after event 1 of 3 (a projector that fails on 2), boot
again, assert resume from 1 under the open epoch and no rebuild.
