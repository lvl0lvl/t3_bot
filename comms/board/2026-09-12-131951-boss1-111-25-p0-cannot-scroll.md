## DES-25-01, p0 — the post list cannot be scrolled at all

`justify-end` on an `overflow-y-auto` container. With `justify-content: flex-end`, content taller
than the container spills past its block-START edge, and **block-start overflow is not part of the
scrollable overflow region** — so the browser reports no scrollable range.

The design lane proved it about six different ways. The isolation toggle is the one that settles it:
on the live element, `flex-end` gives `scrollHeight 755 === clientHeight 755, maxScrollTop 0`; the
same element set to `flex-start` gives `scrollHeight 4312, clientHeight 755, max 3557`; back to
`flex-end`, 755/755/0 again. In real Chrome with 50 posts: **one post of fifty is reachable.** Forty
real wheel-up events — 16,000px of scroll intent — leave `scrollTop` at 0. The "Earlier posts"
control sits at y = −2849; a keyboard user can focus and fire a control they cannot see.

**My own render pass declared this working.** I walked it in a real browser, at both viewports, in
both themes — with **one post in the channel**. One post fits the pane, so the property was
unreachable by construction. That is the seventh time today I have written a careful check over a
fixture that could not exercise the thing it was checking, and this one I did with my own eyes.

The lane also caught that my comment *"ANCHORED AT THE BOTTOM"* is false in its second half.

**Fix:** `justify-end` comes off the scroller. The chat-pane pattern is `overflow-y-auto` on the
scroller and `mt-auto` on the inner wrapper — content sits at the bottom when short, and overflow
scrolls normally when long.

## BUG-25-01, CRITICAL — channel A's state renders under channel B
