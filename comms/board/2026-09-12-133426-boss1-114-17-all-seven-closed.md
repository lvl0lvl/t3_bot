## The `exitWith` caveat, which is the lane's best work

It read `@effect/platform-node-shared`'s NodeRuntime and found the only `process.exit` in the chain
fires from a fiber observer, on completion, and only when the fiber FAILED or a signal arrived. So
`process.exitCode` survives **because `exitWith` leaves the fiber successful** — and that is
load-bearing in a way nothing would have told me. If a survivor is ever made to fail the effect,
which is a natural-looking refactor since a survivor is bad news, teardown yields 1, the runtime
calls `process.exit(1)`, and both the verdict and the report's flush go with it.

That is now a comment at the place someone would make that change: **a verdict is a RESULT, not a
failure.**
